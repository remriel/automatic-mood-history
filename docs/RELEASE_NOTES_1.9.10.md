## Automatic Mood History 1.9.10

- Composition over time shows up to ten of the most common emotions in the selected date range. Shorter or custom ranges can surface emotions that are uncommon across the full history.
- Colors follow the emotion across date ranges, with gold for joy, red for anger, blue for sadness, and a consistent color for each custom label. The legend, chart, and Common Emotions bars use the same assignments.

This display update uses your existing mood history. No reanalysis is needed.
