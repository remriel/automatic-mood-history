# Smaller summary cards

The five mood summary cards are approximately 30% smaller, leaving more space for the dashboard. The cards continue to wrap on narrow screens and support enlarged text. Scores and calculations are unchanged.

Common Emotions bars now match the composition chart colors for the same emotion.

The four dashboard toolbar buttons are more compact, with touch-friendly targets on phones.

The 7-day, 14-day and other time-range buttons are about 30% smaller on desktop, while remaining touch-friendly on phones.
