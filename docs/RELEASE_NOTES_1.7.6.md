# Automatic Mood History 1.7.6

Common Emotions now uses the selected trend range, matching Days scored and the average cards. This applies to the default window, every preset, and custom date ranges. Selecting an empty range clears the emotion bars, and returning to a preset restores its counts.

Saved records and full history are unchanged. No reanalysis is required.

## Composition over time

A new stacked percentage chart shows the mix of recorded emotions across the selected range, similar to Morning Pages composition. It displays the eight most common emotions plus Other emotions, groups long ranges into up to 16 calendar periods, and leaves periods without recorded emotions blank. Period details provide exact counts and percentages. Shares describe recorded labels, not emotional intensity.
