# Automatic Mood History 1.7.0 — Explore your trends

The trend chart now gives your mood history room to breathe and makes individual days easier to inspect.

- **Read each score at a glance:** mood, energy, connection, and intensity have separate aligned plots. The summary also includes average intensity.
- **See time accurately:** horizontal spacing reflects calendar time, including gaps between recorded days. Lines connect observations; they are not estimates of missing days.
- **Focus on a window:** choose 30 days, 90 days, 1 year (365 days), or all history. Windows end on the latest scored day and include it.
- **Explore a day:** use the keyboard- and touch-friendly slider or select the chart to reveal the date and all four scores.
- **Keep your view:** existing saved series visibility remains in place. The selected window and day reset on dashboard refresh. Summary cards and history remain unfiltered.

## Install or update

Use [BRAT](https://github.com/TfTHacker/obsidian42-brat) with `https://github.com/remriel/automatic-mood-history`, or copy `main.js`, `manifest.json`, and `styles.css` into `.obsidian/plugins/automatic-mood-history/`. The optional `moodring-model.bin` goes in the same folder. Restart Obsidian and enable the plugin.

This release does not change saved scores or analysis settings. Scores are inferences, not medical assessments. Desktop and mobile browser emulation are tested; physical-device and native Obsidian verification are not part of this release check.
