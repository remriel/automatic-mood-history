# Automatic Mood History 1.2.7

Click Mood, Energy, Connection, or Intensity in the chart legend to show or hide that series and its points. Choices persist across redraws, refreshes, and restarts, and remain synchronized across open charts.

Buttons support Tab, Space, and Enter with accessible pressed states. An explanatory message appears when every series is hidden. Failed settings writes restore the previous selection. Scores, history records, and analysis behavior are unchanged.

Verified with keyboard interactions, per-series marks and traces, all-hidden state, refresh/resize persistence, save-failure recovery, record preservation, and existing responsive light/dark layout and plugin tests.
